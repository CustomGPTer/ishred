What IShred does not do

IShred does not wipe the whole phone or reach other apps' private data, and it does not claim any certification. It is not on Google Play, so it does not update itself.

Where the others do more

iShredder has an independent app-security assessment and named standard methods such as DoD 5220.22-M. Shreddit offers named national standards and takes files shared to it from other apps. Both install and update from Google Play.
