Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the IShred app for Android (package com.jonjackson.ishred) and this website. IShred is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • IShred does not hold Android's internet permission. It cannot go online, so it cannot send anything anywhere.
  • It works on your files where they are and keeps no copies of them.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

IShred keeps its settings, the app lock check value, any presets you save and its session reports in one small file in its private storage, encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key held in Android's Keystore. File names and locations are left out of reports unless you switch them on. Android's cloud backup is switched off for IShred. Free-space overwrite writes temporary random data in IShred's own folder and removes it afterwards.

Reports

PDF and JSON reports are made only when you ask, and saved where you choose. Once saved, they are yours to keep, share or delete.

Links to this website

Settings, About has Check for a newer version, More apps and Website. Each opens a web page in your browser; IShred itself never goes online.

Keeping and deleting your data

Clear report history in Reports. Uninstalling IShred, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes everything it holds. Neither touches your own files.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts beyond the app list. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.
